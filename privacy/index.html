2. This website

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other details on this website, and we do not set cookies. Specifically:

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). To deliver a page, GitHub and its content delivery network receive your IP address and standard request details such as the page asked for, the time, and your browser type. GitHub keeps these in server logs for security and operations under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or have access to those logs.
  • Fonts. Some pages load the typeface Inter from Google Fonts (Google LLC). Your browser then contacts Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. This page does not; it serves the font itself.
  • Storage in your browser. The tools on this site save your own tool settings and setlists in your browser's local storage, and the home page saves one flag noting that you have tried its touch instrument. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be removed by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Microphone and files. The tuner asks your browser for microphone access, and the key and tempo tool reads an audio file you pick. Both are processed inside your browser only. No audio leaves your device.
  • No tracking. There are no analytics tools, advertising pixels, social plug-ins or fingerprinting on this website. We do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals because there is no tracking to switch off.

8. Your rights in the EU/EEA

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive data you provided in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work.

See how to make a request. We answer within one month; this can be extended by two further months for complex requests, and we will tell you if so. There is no charge.

10. Requesting access or deletion

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask for a copy, have it corrected, or have it deleted.

  • Email privacy@allinc.fi and say what you are asking for, for example "access" or "delete my data".
  • Tell us which app or test the request concerns and the email address you used, so we can find your data. Do not send passwords.
  • We may ask for a detail we already hold to confirm it is you. We then act on the request and tell you when it is done, within the time limits in sections 8 and 9. Data we must keep by law is kept only for that purpose, and we will tell you if that applies.

Website. We hold no data about website visitors, so there is nothing for us to send or delete. To remove the toolbox settings stored in your own browser, clear site data for allinc.fi in your browser settings.

TestFlight. To stop taking part, open the app's page in TestFlight and choose Stop Testing. Data Apple holds about your Apple Account is managed through Apple.
