2. This website

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other details on this website, and we do not set cookies. Specifically:

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). To deliver a page, GitHub and its content delivery network receive your IP address and standard request details such as the page asked for, the time, and your browser type. GitHub keeps these in server logs for security and operations under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or have access to those logs.
  • Fonts. Some pages load the typeface Inter from Google Fonts (Google LLC). Your browser then contacts Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. This page does not; it serves the font itself.
  • Storage in your browser. The toolbox on the home page saves your own tool settings and setlists in your browser's local storage, and the home page saves one flag noting that you have tried its touch instrument. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be removed by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Microphone and files. The tuner asks your browser for microphone access, and the key and tempo tool reads an audio file you pick. Both are processed inside your browser only. No audio leaves your device.
  • No tracking. There are no analytics tools, advertising pixels, social plug-ins or fingerprinting on this website. We do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals because there is no tracking to switch off.
